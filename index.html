You start on pole, 28 m behind the line; the first crossing starts lap 1. The gearbox shifts itself and keyboard easy mode is on, so you can just steer, accelerate and brake. Turn both off under Assists when you want the full car. Tyres come out of blankets at about 70 °C and grip best near 90 °C; brakes work best between 350 and 700 °C.

Share of braking torque at the front. Around 68% the fronts lock just before the rears in a hard stop. Lower values rotate the car on entry but can lock the rears and spin it.
Higher preload holds the rear together on power but pushes mid-corner.
More front share means more understeer; less means a looser rear.
Burned as you drive (about 4 L a lap here); a lighter car is quicker. Applies when you restart from the grid.
Each step adds rear downforce and drag, and moves the aero balance rearward.

When you steer with the keyboard, easy mode adds stability control (it brakes single wheels to stop a slide), automatic countersteer, strong traction control, ABS and a gentler throttle. It turns itself off when you drive with a gamepad or wheel.

0 is off. Higher levels cut power at lower rear wheelspin.

Steering assist limits how far keys and sticks can turn the wheels at speed, to about the slip angle that gives peak grip. It never applies to a bound wheel.

A lower value that matches your screen size gives a truer sense of speed and distance.
